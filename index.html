Water Quality Index (WQI) Equation

Weighted Arithmetic Index Equation:

$$WQI = 100 - \left( \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{n} Q_i \cdot W_i}{\sum_{i=1}^{n} W_i} \times 0.5 \right)$$

Where \(Q_i = \frac{V_i - V_{ideal}}{S_i - V_{ideal}} \times 100\), \(V_i\) is actual parameter value, and \(S_i\) is standard permissible limit.

Parameter BIS IS:10500 Guidelines

  • pH (6.5 - 8.5): Hydrogen ion concentration indicator.
  • TDS (< 500 mg/L): Total Dissolved Inorganic & Organic mineral salts.
  • Hardness (< 300 mg/L): \(Ca^{2+}\) and \(Mg^{2+}\) mineral scaling factor.
  • Coliform (0 MPN/100ml): Biological pathogen indicator.
